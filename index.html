Rugswise ↗
Problem:
Iranian handmade rugs are one of the most intricate crafts in the world, and where a rug comes from determines most of its value. But the knowledge to identify provenance, weaving techniques, knot density, regional patterns, has always lived in the heads of a few experts, scattered and inaccessible. As an Iranian, I grew up around rugs and wanted to preserve both the craft and the knowledge around it.
Solution:
A provenance identification tool backed by a dataset I assembled of rugs from regions across Iran, annotated with weaving characteristics, material composition, and regional markers. The model takes an image and structured inputs and predicts origin, helping buyers, collectors, and inheritors understand what they actually have.
Joinifyouwant
Problem:
Always out of sync with friends for activities. We'd want to play badminton and need four people, but pinging everyone last-minute was painful enough that we'd just end up playing alone. And at some point we realised we didn't have time to see all our friends separately anymore.
Solution:
An app where anyone in your circle can post a last-minute activity and leave it open for others to join. You see what's happening, you show up if you want. It solved the team-forming problem and turned activities into a way to actually see friends, without scheduling a separate dinner two weeks out.
